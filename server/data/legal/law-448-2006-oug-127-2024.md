# Romania: Law 448/2006 on persons with disabilities (as amended, including OUG 127/2024)

> Informational summary, not legal advice. Last checked: 22 October 2026.
> Sources: Legea 448/2006, consolidated text on [legislatie.just.ro](https://legislatie.just.ro/) (search "Legea 448/2006");
> OUG 127/2024 (Monitorul Oficial no. 1124, 11 November 2024); joint order ANPDPD–ANOFM on Article 78 (Monitorul Oficial no. 68, 28 January 2025);
> commentary: [Capital.ro on OUG 127/2024](https://www.capital.ro/angajarea-persoanelor-cu-dizabilitati-va-avea-noua-procedura-din-oug-127-2024-o-eficienta-sporita.html).
> The law has been amended many times, most recently relevant to employers by **OUG 127/2024**.
> **Always check the current consolidated text before relying on figures or deadlines.**

## Main points for employers

- **Reasonable accommodation:** employers must adapt the workplace and working conditions so that an employee with a disability can work,
  as part of the law's rules on employment and the definition of "adaptare rezonabilă".
- **Employment quota:** public and private employers with **at least 50 employees** must employ persons with disabilities
  in a proportion of **at least 4%** of total staff (Article 78).
- **OUG 127/2024** (in force for this obligation from **1 January 2025**) made the quota harder to avoid:
  - an employer that has to hire must send a written request for support to **at least three NGOs** that provide services
    for persons with disabilities, within **10 days** of the date the obligation arises;
  - employers report the positions held by persons with disabilities to the authorities **once a year**;
  - according to commentary on the ordinance, paying the monthly contribution to the state budget is no longer enough on its own:
    the employer must also show it **tried to hire**.
- The detailed procedure for Article 78 paragraphs (2²)–(2⁴) is in the joint ANPDPD–ANOFM order published in January 2025.
- Persons with disabilities have the right to request **adjustments to working time** and to training and career support.

## What it means for Brightline

- Brightline's documents mention 34 engineers but not the total headcount. If the company has 50 or more employees,
  the 4% quota applies to it. The F9 audit reports this as "no evidence found in the repo/docs", not as a violation.

## What it means for a junior developer

- Romanian law, not only EU law, requires your employer to adapt your workplace when you need it.
- You can ask for adjustments without describing your diagnosis to your team.
