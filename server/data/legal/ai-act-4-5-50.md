# EU AI Act: Articles 4, 5 and 50

> Informational summary, not legal advice. Last checked: 22 October 2026.
> Source: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj).

## Article 4: AI literacy (applies since 2 February 2025)

Providers and deployers of AI systems must take measures to ensure a **sufficient level of AI literacy** of their staff
and other people using AI systems on their behalf, taking into account their knowledge and the context of use.

## Article 5: Prohibited practices (applies since 2 February 2025)

Among others, it is prohibited to place on the market or use AI systems that:

- **exploit the vulnerabilities** of a person or group due to their age, **disability** or social or economic situation,
  in a way that materially distorts their behaviour and causes or is likely to cause significant harm (Article 5(1)(b));
- **infer emotions of a person in the workplace** or in education, except for medical or safety reasons (Article 5(1)(f)).

## Article 50: Transparency (applies from 2 August 2026)

- People must be **informed that they are interacting with an AI system**, unless it is obvious from the context.
- AI-generated text, audio, image and video content must be marked as AI-generated in a machine-readable way
  (with exceptions, for example when the AI only assists with standard editing).

## Related: high-risk systems in employment (Annex III, point 4)

AI systems used for recruitment, for decisions on promotion or termination, for allocating tasks based on personal traits,
or for **monitoring and evaluating the performance and behaviour** of workers are **high-risk** and carry heavy obligations.

## How OnRamp follows it

- The **About AI** page (`/about-ai`) and labels in the app say when an answer, summary or letter is AI-generated (Article 50).
- OnRamp does **not** monitor or evaluate employees, recognise emotions or rank people. F3 and F12 extract only what concerns
  the person who asks, at their request. F4 explains review conventions, not the reviewer's mood (Article 5(1)(f), Annex III).
- Nothing is sent to the manager automatically.
