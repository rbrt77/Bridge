# UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, Article 27: Work and employment

> Informational summary, not legal advice. Last checked: 22 October 2026.
> Source: [CRPD, Article 27](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/convention-on-the-rights-of-persons-with-disabilities/article-27-work-and-employment.html).
> Romania ratified the Convention through Law 221/2010. The EU is also a party.

## What it says

- Persons with disabilities have the right to work **on an equal basis with others**, in a work environment that is open, inclusive and accessible.
- States must prohibit discrimination on the basis of disability in **all matters of employment**: recruitment, hiring, continuance of employment, career advancement, and safe and healthy working conditions.
- States must ensure that **reasonable accommodation** is provided to persons with disabilities in the workplace (Article 27(1)(i)).
- Persons with disabilities should have access to training, career guidance and job placement, and to opportunities for career advancement.

## What "reasonable accommodation" means (Article 2)

Necessary and appropriate modification and adjustments that do not impose a disproportionate or undue burden,
where needed in a particular case, so that a person with a disability can exercise their rights on an equal basis with others.
**Denying reasonable accommodation is a form of discrimination** under the Convention.

## What it means for a junior developer

- You can ask for changes to how, when or with what tools you work, so you can do the same job as your colleagues.
- You do not have to accept being left out of career steps (reviews, promotions, training) because of a disability.

## How OnRamp uses it
