# Brightline Software · Workplace Adjustments Policy

Version 1.0 · Owner: People team · Approved: March 2024

## 1. Purpose

Brightline wants everyone to be able to do their best work. Some colleagues need changes to
their equipment, schedule or way of working to do that. This policy explains how to ask for
those changes ("adjustments").

## 2. Who can ask

Any employee can ask for an adjustment, at any time, including during the hiring process and
the probation period. You do not need to have a formal disability certificate to ask.

## 3. Examples of adjustments

- Equipment: an ergonomic chair, a standing desk, a second monitor, a different keyboard or mouse.
- Schedule: different start and end times, more breaks, part-time work for a period.
- Place: working from home more often, a quieter desk in the office.
- Meetings: receiving the agenda earlier, written notes after the meeting.
- Tasks: changing some tasks between team members, with the manager's agreement.

This list is not complete. You can ask for something that is not on it.

## 4. How to ask

1. Talk to your manager, or write to the People team at people@brightline.example if you prefer
   not to talk to your manager first.
2. Explain what makes your work harder and what change would help.
3. Your manager and the People team look at the request together. They may invite you to a short
   meeting to understand it better.
4. In some cases we may ask for a document from a doctor.
5. You will receive an answer from your manager or the People team.

## 5. Costs

Brightline pays for approved adjustments. Equipment stays the property of Brightline and goes with
you if you change teams.

## 6. If the situation changes

Adjustments can be changed when your needs or your role change. Talk to your manager or the People team.

## 7. Questions

Write to people@brightline.example or ask in `#ask-people` on Slack.
